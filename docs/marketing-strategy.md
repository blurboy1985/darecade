# Play Simi: organic marketing strategy

Prepared 11 October 2026. Launch window: 12 October–10 November 2026.

Audience: global, English-speaking. Marketing spend: S$0. Recommended workload: about seven hours a week for one person.

## Recommendation

Build the first audience through short gameplay videos, relevant gaming communities and a weekly hosted play session. Create four original vertical videos a week and adapt each for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Use Play Simi's Singapore-inspired neighbourhood as the visual signature, with racing, rhythm play and avatar expression as the reasons to try it.

The first-month objective is to find a repeatable way to attract people who finish a round and return. Purchases and follower totals are secondary at this stage.

## Positioning and audience

Public name: **Play Simi**. Main address: https://playsimi.danielquek.com. Keep the existing tagline, **Meet you downstairs.**

Suggested core message:

> A little neighbourhood for your next game night. Race, dance, explore and make your character your own — free to play in your browser.

Use “Singapore-inspired neighbourhood” as a supporting detail. Show its playground, plaza and everyday scenery, while keeping headlines and instructions understandable without Singapore knowledge.

Start with three audience hypotheses:

| Audience | Reason to try | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| Casual players and pairs of friends, initially adults 18+ | A quick activity together without installing a game | A close kart finish; friends joining a race; a dance party |
| Cozy-game and avatar-customisation fans | A pleasant place and a character to make their own | Outfit changes, pets, fishing, the playground, evening lighting |
| Browser-game and indie-game enthusiasts | An original game they can try and discuss immediately | A strong gameplay moment, followed by a short creator explanation |

These are hypotheses to test, not verified audience demographics. Adult-focused initial recruitment also gives room to resolve the current world-chat moderation review before planning a teen-focused campaign. The game's 13+ eligibility is separate from this marketing audience choice.

YouTube says URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are non-clickable; channel profile links are clickable. Do not make a pinned Shorts URL the only route to play. [YouTube link guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en).

TikTok's published guidance says website links require at least 1,000 followers or a Registered Business Account, and some linking features vary by location. Check the actual account before writing “link in bio.” Do not change account type solely on an assumption that a link will appear. [TikTok profile-link guidance](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/linking-another-social-media-account).

Reddit prohibits repetitive mass promotion; each community can impose additional rules. Community selection and its current posting rules must be checked before posting. [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam).

## Content system

Use approximately 60% action and challenge clips, 25% neighbourhood/character clips, and 15% creator or feedback clips. Record real gameplay, export a clean master without another platform's watermark, add readable English captions, and use audio whose rights cover each destination.

A useful 15-second structure:

1. **0–2 seconds:** the interesting moment plus a short hook.
2. **2–10 seconds:** the attempt, mistake or transformation.
3. **10–13 seconds:** the payoff: an overtake, a Perfect, a catch or the finished outfit.
4. **13–15 seconds:** one action: “Play free in your browser” or “Bring a friend for one race.”

Show one reason to play per video. Keep the full neighbourhood montage as a profile introduction or trailer, while testing shorter clips for discovery.

| Week | Four source-video ideas |
|---|---|
| 1 | “I only needed one more corner.” — Coast Rush finish; “A tiny neighbourhood in your browser.” — exploration; “Pick your game-night look.” — outfit change; “Can I get three Perfects?” — Open Stage |
| 2 | “The coconut ruined everything.” — race reversal; “Come for one game. Stay to explore.” — playground/pet; “This basket had no right to go in.” — Hoops; a short creator invitation to the hosted session |
| 3 | A genuine friend-versus-friend race; a Stage Party highlight; a fishing catch with a fast payoff; “You asked for…” — a real improvement, or an honest answer if nothing changed |
| 4 | A new cut of the strongest racing idea; a new cut of the strongest cozy/character idea; a consented community highlight; “Meet you downstairs this weekend.” — session invitation |

Use only activities present in the current release. Recapture the newer multiplayer racing and Stage Party features. The existing 15-second vertical, 30-second vertical and 30-second landscape promo files are useful starting assets, but their October 7 capture notes predate newer features and some current flows. Check each cut before publishing.

The older plaza footage uses helper guest sessions. It can demonstrate the game, but must not be presented as evidence of an existing crowd or community popularity. Obtain permission before featuring identifiable player contributions; keep private account details out of footage.

## Acquisition and return loop

**Gameplay clip → game visit → normal eligibility/consent → first completed round → another round → a return visit → a friend completes a first round.**

Send people to the game directly, rather than adding a multi-link page unless multiple destinations become necessary. Email saving stays optional. Make the next action obvious and match the clip's promise: a racing clip should explain how to start Coast Rush; a dancing clip should explain how to find Open Stage.

An example campaign link is:

`https://playsimi.danielquek.com/?intro=0&utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=global_launch_oct2026&utm_content=coast_overtake_v1`

`intro=0` is an existing way to skip the film; it does not bypass eligibility or consent. The campaign tags describe the source; they do not select an activity or create analytics by themselves. Do not advertise unimplemented activity deep links.

After a satisfying completed round, use the existing friend-invite flow: a new friend's first verified round gives both players 30 XP, with the inviter's reward capped at five friends. Keep the reward wording accurate. Measure completed friend rounds rather than copies of the link.

Host one 30–45-minute play session weekly. Start with 10–20 invited participants and a named activity, rather than a vague invitation to an empty world. Alternate initial session trials between Saturday **08:00 SGT / 00:00 UTC** and Saturday **21:00 SGT / 13:00 UTC**, then keep the time with stronger actual attendance. These test different global windows; they are not claims about a universal best time. Only announce sessions the creator can host and moderate.

No marketing emails should be sent to game-account addresses unless those people separately opted into marketing.

Suggested weekly time budget: 90 minutes capturing gameplay; two hours editing four clips; one hour uploading/adapting; 90 minutes for community replies and creator research; 45 minutes hosting; 15 minutes reviewing the scorecard. Adjust production quality or optional channels before increasing hours.

Creator outreach is a planned action only. No messages, posts, listings or sessions have been sent, published or scheduled as part of preparing this strategy.

## Measurement and decision rules

The repository search did not find an obvious analytics SDK, campaign-tag handler or event-tracking integration. This is a planning gap, not proof that no external reporting exists. Audit the available reporting before claiming attribution or retention.

Minimum future measurement should capture campaign source, eligibility/consent completion, first verified round, second round, subsequent-day play and invite first-round completion. Use the existing privacy approach and minimal identifiers. Avoid adding advertising trackers just to run this experiment.

Track anonymous/profile-based retention consistently; do not call browser profiles unique people. Link sharing, cleared storage, cross-device use and platform browser changes can distort attribution. If measurement is not implemented, use a consented manual playtest cohort and clearly label broad marketing conversion and retention as unknown.

| Measure | Definition | Initial decision aid |
|---|---|---|
| Activated profiles | New profiles with a first verified completed round | Aim for 100 in month one once reliable counts exist. This is a planning target, not a forecast. |
| First-round completion | New eligible profiles completing a round / new profiles completing consent | Investigate below 40% after at least 50 eligible new profiles; compare by device and source. |
| Same-session replay | Activated profiles starting another round / activated profiles | Investigate below 30%; inspect result-screen clarity and the actual desire to replay. |
| Day-7 return | Activated profiles playing again on the seventh subsequent Singapore calendar day / activated profiles whose day-7 window has elapsed | Tentative target 15–20%; report numerator, denominator and day definition. Small cohorts are directional only. |
| Successful referrals | Invited new profiles completing a first verified round | Aim for 10 in month one; inspect the funnel if links are copied but friends do not play. |
| Effort efficiency | Activated or returning profiles attributable to a channel / hours spent on it | Use this to choose month-two channels; compare like-for-like measurement windows. |

Views, completion rates on videos, shares and follower growth help diagnose creative performance. They do not substitute for game activation or retention. Compare comparable clips within each platform because view definitions and distribution differ.

After four weeks, concentrate on the two channel/creative combinations that produce the strongest activated and returning cohorts. If reliable game measurement is still missing, make that the next priority instead of declaring a winning acquisition channel from video views alone.

## Launch preparation and later options

Before directing larger traffic spikes, check the real mobile/in-app-browser route, first-round flow, friend-room joining and current hosting headroom. Existing hosting documentation warns that free-tier capacity is workload-dependent; start with small sessions and observe actual failures/usage rather than promising scale.

The current privacy review identifies specific gaps in recently added typed world chat. Resolve message reporting/moderation and the notice review before a teen-focused or broadly unattended community push. This is a concrete product-readiness item, not a claim that an adult audience removes those responsibilities.

Prepare one small press kit: a two-sentence description, creator contact, logo, five current screenshots, the 15-second vertical trailer and a short controls/feature sheet. Add social preview metadata and a clear share image when implementation work is undertaken; neither Open Graph nor social-card metadata appears in the inspected HTML. These are recommendations, not changes made by this plan.

Defer paid advertising, a large standalone Discord, daily long-form videos and broad PR outreach. An organic creator feature or small article can be useful after the game has evidence of repeat play; none is assumed to be free or guaranteed.

Treat itch.io and larger game portals as distribution projects, not simple link submissions. [itch.io's HTML5 documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5) describes its browser-game packaging and iframe environment. Play Simi's same-origin APIs, cookies, multiplayer and checkout need specific compatibility testing.

CrazyGames is a later assessment: its requirements affect external logins, multiplayer, purchases and promotion of playable external versions. Do not assume the existing site can be uploaded unchanged or used mainly to send players back to the main website. [CrazyGames integration requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/intro/), [gameplay requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/gameplay/).

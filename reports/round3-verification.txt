PHASES SHIPPED
1. Stability: removed the background-401 page reload; retain the running game and accept its returned progress. Fixed-step race time uses a monotonic timestamp and a nondecreasing display. The initial limit comes from the same race configuration. W taps/holds use the movement latch. BEST uses recorded ticks. Removed the global pointerdown cancellation. Idle clients send four-second heartbeats; the server expires stale presence after 12 seconds, checked every three seconds. Offline social cards no longer become live world avatars.
2. Variety: append-only hair IDs 53–132 provide eight shapes in ten colours. Existing IDs 0–52 retain their mappings. New guest identities are seeded by ID. NPCs avoid occupied hair combinations and limit blue choices; the collision pass also runs when live neighbours join. Added rear clumps/nape points and five iris colours. Existing saved/custom player looks remain intact.
3. Life: natural blinks use per-character 3–5 second periods with 120 ms fully closed; breathing now survives skeleton posing. Added weight shift/look-around and restored emote faces. NPCs wander among fixed points of interest independently of the player, with 2.05 m visual separation. NPC labels fade on overlap; the player's highlighted label and ring remain visible.
4. Classes/wardrobe: shared curved cape geometry widens toward the hem, with collars and clasps and existing sway. Pirate gets a volumetric tricorn and larger cutlass; Thief gets a neck scarf and larger daggers. Try-on updates only the preview/actions and turns to the front, preserving the item-list DOM and scroll.
5. Polish: upfront guest XP and separate email-saving copy, no 'Verified round'. Finished-race nameplates are hidden and quit titles sized to avoid overlap. Automatic performance fallback announces itself once; Preferences offers a return to full quality. Portraits use serialized asynchronous capture and a bounded cache. HTML no-transform prevents automatic Cloudflare beacon injection. Phone opening view includes Daily Board and Mama Shop and eases closer on movement. Building walk-to-open already worked and was verified without changing that logic.

It also checks the persisted player look and computed Style-token balance. The final suite passed this test. New hair IDs pass normalisation and wearability tests; old item IDs are not removed. LIMITS / NOT CLAIMED
- Physical mid-range-phone 60 fps, under-100-ms input and 4G startup targets were not benchmarked. Responsive layout was checked in Chrome at 390 x 844.
- The blue-hair cap and collision rerolls apply to generated NPCs/default review identities. Real players can deliberately choose matching saved hair; their owned/custom looks are not silently rewritten. Thus the strict 'any screenshot of ten arbitrary live players' condition is not guaranteed.
- Browser checks cover the interactions listed above, not every possible button, viewport, emote timing or GPU. Automatic low-FPS toast was implemented but not induced with a measured slow physical device.
- This work does not establish a subjective 9–10/10 visual score.

WHAT CHANGED
Phase 1: Removed button focus traps and hover hit-target movement; short movement
presses survive the interval between frames. Opening panels no longer rebuilds
the avatar. NPC selection targets the body/name, not surrounding effects. Level-up
toasts wait for panels to close. Menu fits short landscape screens.

Phase 2: Stable seeded guest/NPC appearances cover eight hair shapes and colours,
skin tones and four iris colours. Larger hair masses, clearer portrait framing,
blinking and existing idle motion. Hats avoid inverted-hull brim artifacts.
NPC/remote rendering uses minimum spacing and staggered name labels.

Phase 3: Contrasting class capes, larger back weapons, archer hood/quiver, pirate
coat tails and thief scarf tails. Equip/emotes briefly face the camera. Cheap
shader dithering fades obstructing scenery instead of pushing the camera into
the player's head. Phone framing includes Arcade Row and the Daily Board.

Phase 4: Until a completed Palm Bay race, a 180-second novice mode assists against
AI. Novice races grant XP/Play medals but are unranked; subsequent races retain
normal difficulty. Negative run seeds distinguish novice replays without a save
schema change. Countdown freezes race time; fixed simulation ticks are monotonic.
Escape pauses with Resume/Quit. Quit produces an incomplete result. No-finish
results do not celebrate. Progress feedback and race-view preloading added.

Phase 5: Initial live presence and HTTP fallback avoid transient disconnect copy.
Quiet-world copy mentions neighbours. Local-clock automatic lighting is the new
default. Final guest names and medal totals render from the profile immediately.
Hoops/Fishing card art, Dragon Rooftop presentation, correct Arcade Row stage
label, planter-safe navigation, adult-only donation UI and server enforcement,
clearer guest rewards, natural-height wardrobe cards and compact phone HUD.
The deployed page contains no Cloudflare Insights beacon or external tracker.

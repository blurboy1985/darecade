# Character asset credits

Darecade's connected body topology, humanoid skeleton and four base hairstyles are adapted from **Universal Base Characters — Standard** by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The original licence is retained in `art/characters/source/Universal Base Characters[Standard]/License_Standard.txt`.

Darecade's rounded face, eyes, proportion edits, tailored clothing, shoes, curtain locks and motion retargeting were authored for this project. The user's supplied image guided the cute proportions and styling. This asset is not a Tripo export.

Editable master: `art/characters/neighbour-cute.blend`.
Builder: `scripts/build_cute_character.py`.
Runtime: `public/models/neighbour.glb` (all wardrobe variants; one outfit and hairstyle render at a time).

The earlier `neighbour.blend` and `build_character.py` are retained as the previous rigid-part prototype, not the active asset builder.

The repository includes the 24 source model, texture and licence files needed by the active character builder. The full downloaded ZIP, unused kit variants, temporary import scene and automatic Blender backups remain local.
